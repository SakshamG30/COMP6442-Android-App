## Meeting Minutes
- **Project Requirement Confirmation**: 
    - All members confirmed and ensured that they have understood the complete written requirements of the Group Project details.
- **Topic Confirmation**: 
    - The 2.5 hour meeting mostly revolved around topic confirmation by ensuring we all agree on the same page and understand the feasibility of it.
- **Exploration of Goals**: 
    - We explored all the 34 goals proposed by the UN and the Council of Australian Government in depth to finalize our topic. The exploration was based on practicality and application of what’s required for the assignment, personal interest and long term planning of the Software even after the course ends. 
    - The goals that we talked the most about were ‘Quality Education’, ‘Zero Hunger’ and ‘Sustainable Cities and Communities’ from UN’s Sustainable Development Goals, and ‘Everyone enjoys long and healthy lives’, ‘Students achieve their full learning potential’, ‘Students reach their full potential through further education pathways’, ‘Youth are engaged in employment or education’, ‘Strong economic participation and development of people and their communities’, ‘Families and households are safe’, ‘People enjoy high levels of social and emotional wellbeing’ and ‘People have access to information and services enabling participation in informed decision-making regarding their own lives’ but Closing The Gap. 
    - We chose ‘People enjoy high levels of social and emotional wellbeing’.
- **Selection Criteria**: 
    - Our selection criteria was finalized based on our end goal of Community and Access through the Software, understanding the audience that would use the app, acknowledging which goal would have a practical demand and acknowledging the audience for each goal. We considered user participation, demand for new joiners, word-of-mouth for user growth, user engagement probability, access to public information, feasiblility of access to help, and above all making the app meaningful and outstanding as a software.
- **Privacy and Consent**: 
    - Understanding public and private engagement between the software users, and acknowledging Consent at every stage given the sensitivity of the Goal (consent, privacy + our assurance to users). We discussed the management of individual user privacy and their Filters on searching and being discovered/reached.
- **Real time data update**: 
    - Considered mechanisms for updating user data in real-time.especially last update of users data etc)
- **Search Feature Dynamics**: 
    - Explored potential search functionalities for information and user discovery.
- **Extended Features and Development**: 
    - We also discussed what extended features could look like and planned developing around hypothetical scenarios

## Decisions Made:
- The group project will focus on ‘People enjoy high levels of social and emotional wellbeing’, emphasizing community access and user engagement.
- Development will consider privacy, consent, and real-time data updates as foundational elements.
